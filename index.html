How this works

  1. Read together The voice reads a line slowly and you read it out loud at the same time. One line at a time, never a wall of text.
  2. Watch the words turn green Your microphone follows along and lights up each word as it hears you say it. That green line is the whole point — it is proof you were understood.
  3. Take as long as you want Nothing ever cuts you off. Stumble, stop, start the line again, then press Done when you are finished. Anything the mic missed gets underlined.

You already know more English than you can say. The gap between knowing and speaking only closes one way — your own voice, out loud, every day. Ten minutes daily beats two hours on a Sunday.

Wear headphones if you can. Without them the microphone hears the app's own voice as well as yours, and your score will look better than it really is. Everything runs in your browser: speech is handled by the browser's own engine and your progress is saved on this device only. Works best in Chrome.
